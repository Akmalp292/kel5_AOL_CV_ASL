About Us

Welcome to PT ASL Detection Jaya-Jaya. Our platform is a real-time American Sign Languange (ASL) detector built to bridge communication gaps for the Deaf Community. Powered by an optimized MobileNetV2 deep learning model, PT ASL Detection Jaya-Jaya accurately identifies ASL gestures using computer vision technology. Our mission is to empower the Deaf Community by providing an accessible and efficient tool for communication, fostering inclusivity and breaking down barriers in everyday interactions. Our mission is to harness the power of technology to create a more inclusive world, where everyone can communicate freely and confidently, regardless of their hearing abilities. We are committed to continuous innovation and improvement, ensuring that our platform remains at the forefront of ASL detection technology, and we look forward to making a positive impact on the lives of those we serve.

Our Vision

Our vision is to create a world where language is no longer a barrier. We believe that technology has the power to revolutionize the way we communicate and interact, and we are committed to harnessing that potential to empower individuals from all walks of life.
